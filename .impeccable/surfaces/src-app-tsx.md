## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a coin-operated arcade machine's control panel, not a mobile-simulator card floating on a page — game screens live inside beveled panel modules with backlit amber accents and physical-meter readouts, refusing the generic dark-navy-dashboard-with-gradient-buttons look every prior pass defaulted to.

OWN-WORLD: Gunmetal #14171c housing; panel-label white #f2f2ee ink; backlit amber #e8b93a for primary actions/credits; credit-counter teal #2fa5a0 for calm/safety elements; a taped "instruction card" chrome strip carries safety/educational copy. Stenciled panel-cap display type over a clean workhorse sans for body/UI. Beveled panel modules, not rounded cards-on-cards.

STORY: The visitor reads this as a real machine standing in front of them — credits, meters, and backlit controls make the fictional wallet and session boundary legible as physical, trustworthy hardware, not an ambiguous digital abstraction. The taped instruction-card chrome signals "read this before you play," distinct from a sterile modal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: the dashboard fills the desktop viewport as a full-bleed machine-panel console, not a centered card in empty space. A physical control cluster (mastery meter, credit counter, session-allowance readout) sits in the top panel; the game selector is a row of backlit module buttons; safety copy sits in a taped instruction-card strip.

FORM: coin-op machine panel — Impeccable's pick, candidate 3 of 7, seed key 6b1ac58f, user-selected over the assigned Sabong odds-board/ledger direction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
